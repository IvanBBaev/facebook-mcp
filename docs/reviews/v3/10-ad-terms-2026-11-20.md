## 1. What changes

1. **Effective date 2026-11-20** (moved from 2026-10-30). Confirmed on P1 and P2 banners and by Meta's statement to Reuters (S1, S3). The P3 banner still shows 30 Oct while its closing line shows 20 Nov — a Meta page inconsistency, not a real conflict.
2. **Acceptance by continued use.** No click-through; using the ads interfaces (which include the **Marketing APIs**, P1 intro) after the date binds the account (P3 §7; S1).
3. **Third-party software clause — new in the ad terms.** P1 bullet 14: "You are solely responsible for the acts or omissions of any third-party software or service that you use or authorize in connection with your use of the Self-Serve Ad Interfaces." P2 has no such sentence (closest: third-party *service provider* terms in §4). P3 §5 carries a matching sentence. **This is the clause that names tools like this server.**
4. **AI features clause.** "AI features may make mistakes and may not always produce accurate, complete, or suitable results"; the advertiser is "solely responsible for and assume[s] any and all risks associated with such AI features" (P1 bullet 14, P3 §5). The separate *Ad Creative Generative AI Terms* are folded into the Self-Serve Ad Terms (S1, S4). P1 also forbids removing AI-generated watermarks.
5. **Automated features no longer described as optional.** P2 §2: such features "are optional, and you may choose to 'opt in' or 'opt out'". P1 bullet 2 describes automated features for "formatting, selection and generation of Ad Creative and selection of audience, placements, objectives" without the optional/opt-out sentence. Whether defaults actually change is **[UNVERIFIED]** — the terms do not say.
6. **Unchanged but wider in reach** (S4): indemnity for "any damages, losses, and expenses of any kind"; liability cap "the greater of one hundred dollars ($100) or the amount you have paid us in the past twelve months"; charges "for any Orders placed on or through your advertising account" (P1, P2); "solely responsible for the Order, Ad Creative, targeting decisions, and ad placements" (P1; P2 says "ad content").
7. **Meta may modify, limit, suspend or discontinue** commercial features at any time without notice (P3 §9). Relevant to API-version churn.
8. **Crawling authorisation** of business-associated domains (P3 §1), applies to any business using Meta business products, including Page-only use (S4). Concerns the operator's website, not this server.
9. **Arbitration (US businesses)**: 30-day opt-out from first acceptance of any version with the clause (S2, S4). Only new entities first accepting on/after 2026-11-20 likely still have a window **[UNVERIFIED]**.
10. Separately, a product-browsing default added to existing ads from 2026-10-28 unless opted out — from a copied advertiser notice, **[UNVERIFIED]** against Meta's primary notice.

## 2. What it means for this server

- The operator is the advertiser. Every campaign, budget change, targeting edit, copy or boost made through the `ads` package is an "Order placed through your advertising account", and the operator pays for it.
- Under item 3, this server **and the LLM agent driving it** are "third-party software … that you use or authorize". From 2026-11-20 their acts and omissions — a wrong budget, a mistargeted boost, a bug — are contractually the operator's, explicitly. The maintainer has no contract with Meta for the operator's account; the MIT licence disclaims warranty and liability.
- Existing controls are the operator's evidence of due care and should be named as such: plan/apply with `plan_id`, out-of-band confirmation for spend, PAUSED-only creation, mandatory `spend_cap_minor` ≤ `FB_ADS_CAMPAIGN_SPEND_CAP_MAX`, `FB_ADS_BUDGET_CEILING`, DSA payor/beneficiary enforcement, countries/ages-only targeting edits, Advantage+ shopping/app edits refused (`src/api/ads-control.ts:287-470`).
- **AI features.** `facebook_boost_post` explicitly opts out of Meta's creative enhancements (`degrees_of_freedom_spec.creative_features_spec.*: OPT_OUT`) and sets `targeting_automation.advantage_audience: 0` (`src/api/ads-create.ts:605-622`). So a boost does not switch on Meta AI features by itself. Copies (`facebook_duplicate_ad_object`, `src/api/ads-copy.ts`) inherit the source object as-is, including any AI features it already had.
- If Meta changes server-side defaults (item 5), an omitted field may mean "on". The boost path is safe because it sets the fields; any future create path must set them too.
- `special_ad_categories: []` (LEG-04) becomes more exposed: item 6 makes the operator "solely responsible for the Order", and the agent chooses what to boost.

## 3. Recommendations

**Blocking (before 1.0, and before the ads package is promoted beyond opt-in):**

1. **LEG-04 attestation** — require an explicit operator attestation that a boosted post is not housing, employment, credit, or social issues/elections/politics; refuse otherwise. Owner: L2.ads.
2. **No new ad path that enables Meta AI features by default.** Any new create/copy path must set creative-enhancement and Advantage+ audience fields explicitly (OPT_OUT / 0) or require a named operator choice, with a test asserting the payload. Owner: L2.ads.

**Non-blocking (docs, D-01 / L2.ads, size S):**

3. README "Your responsibilities as the operator" — add Notice C from review 09 plus this sentence:

   > From 20 November 2026 Meta's Self-Serve Ad Terms make you solely responsible for the acts or omissions of any third-party software you use with the ads APIs — that includes this server and the AI model driving it. Review every plan before you apply it.

4. Ads write-tool descriptions (`src/tools/ads*.ts`) — append one line:

   > Changes are made in the operator's ad account under Meta's Self-Serve Ad Terms; the operator is billed and responsible for the result. Preview with plan, then apply only after the operator approves.

5. Copy tools — add a warning in the plan preview: "The copy inherits the source's Advantage+ / creative-enhancement settings."
6. Update the calendar line in `docs/analysis/v3/README.md` to "2026-11-20, confirmed on Meta's terms pages 2026-10-30".
7. Lawyer Q7 (review 09) is now partly answered by item 3: liability for third-party tools is explicit. Remaining question: whether the operator's warranties extend to the LLM vendor's output used as ad copy.
8. Re-check P1/P3 on 2026-11-20 for last-minute wording changes (Meta said wording would not change, S1).

**Not affected:** read-only ads tools (listings, insights, previews); crawling and arbitration are operator business matters with no code impact.
